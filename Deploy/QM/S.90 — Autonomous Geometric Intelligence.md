A further question emerges:

This paper develops Autonomous Geometric Intelligence (AGI): a speculative theoretical framework in which intelligence is represented as self-organizing information geometry capable of adaptive coordination and recursive reconstruction.

This framework does not propose artificial consciousness, sentience, self-awareness, or a path to general intelligence.

Instead, it studies abstract computational organization and adaptive information systems.

The central proposal:

Intelligence is not only prediction.

Intelligence may also be represented as adaptive organization of accessibility.

This document presents conceptual research rather than a cognitive theory.
