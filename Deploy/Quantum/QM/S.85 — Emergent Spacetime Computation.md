Abstract

Traditional computation assumes a predefined execution environment:

Information moves within fixed structure.

Across the Structural Sector, computation progressively shifted from fixed execution to organization:

→ informational geometry

A further question emerges:

If information becomes sufficiently organized—

can the execution environment itself become an emergent property?

This paper develops Emergent Spacetime Computation (ESC): a speculative theoretical framework in which computational locality, accessibility, and effective structure arise from information organization rather than being predefined.

This framework does not propose that physical spacetime is computation, nor claim a replacement for relativity, quantum mechanics, or existing physics.

Instead, it studies an abstract computational language in which execution structure is treated as an emergent organizational layer.

The central proposal:

Computation is not only executed within structure.

Structure may itself emerge from information organization.

This document presents speculative conceptual research rather than a physical theory.
