Abstract

Quantum circuits are traditionally represented as ordered sequences of discrete gates acting on qubits through layered execution.

As the Structural Sector progressed from operators to geometry, then from geometry to curvature and topology, an alternative interpretation emerged:

a circuit may not be a sequence of operations.

A circuit may be a geometry through which information moves.

This paper develops Geometric Quantum Circuits (GQC): a theoretical framework in which quantum circuits are represented as structured manifolds that organize state evolution.

In this framework:

* qubits become geometric regions,
* gates become trajectories,
* wires become transport pathways,
* depth becomes traversal distance,
* compilation becomes manifold construction.

The central proposal:

A quantum circuit is not executed.

A quantum circuit is traversed.

This document presents a theoretical and mathematical research framework rather than an experimentally demonstrated circuit architecture.
