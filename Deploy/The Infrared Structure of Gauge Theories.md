# Abstract

The discovery that soft theorems, asymptotic symmetries, and memory effects constitute three manifestations of a single infrared (IR) structure—the **Infrared Triangle**—represents one of the most profound conceptual developments in quantum field theory since the establishment of renormalization and effective field theory.

Beginning with the work of Weinberg on soft photons and gravitons and culminating in the modern program initiated by Strominger and collaborators (2013+), it has become clear that gauge theories possess an infinite-dimensional symmetry structure at null infinity. These asymptotic symmetries generate Ward identities that are precisely equivalent to soft emission theorems and are experimentally observable through memory effects.

Despite these advances, the implications for:

* infrared divergences,
* the structure of QED and QCD vacua,
* confinement,
* factorization,
* non-perturbative gauge dynamics,
* black hole information,
* and the definition of scattering itself,

remain largely unexplored.

This white paper develops a unified and formal framework—the **Infrared Completion Program for Gauge Theories (ICPGT)**—which extends the Infrared Triangle into a comprehensive theory of infrared quantum field dynamics.

We propose:

1. A universal Hilbert space of dressed asymptotic states.
2. An infinite-dimensional vacuum bundle generated by asymptotic charges.
3. A non-perturbative infrared renormalization group.
4. An extension of asymptotic symmetries to confining theories.
5. An infrared quantum information framework.
6. A possible resolution of long-standing infrared divergences in gauge theories.

The central thesis is:

> The infrared sector of gauge theories is not a pathology of perturbation theory. It is an emergent dynamical layer of QFT possessing its own degrees of freedom, symmetries, vacuum structure, and information content.
