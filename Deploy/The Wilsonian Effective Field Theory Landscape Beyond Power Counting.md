# Abstract

Wilsonian Effective Field Theory (EFT) organizes physics below a cutoff scale Λ through a derivative and operator expansion,

[
\mathcal L_{\rm EFT}
=\sum_i C_i(\Lambda),\mathcal O_i .
]

Conventional EFT assumes that Wilson coefficients (C_i) can be computed by perturbatively integrating out heavy fields and arranging operators by canonical mass dimension. This paradigm has been extraordinarily successful for weakly coupled ultraviolet (UV) completions.

However, for strongly coupled UV theories, the mapping

[
{\text{UV dynamics}}
\rightarrow
{C_i}
]

is essentially a black box.

Examples include:

* Confining gauge theories
* Strongly coupled conformal sectors
* Composite Higgs models
* Quantum gravity completions
* Non-Lagrangian quantum field theories
* Topological phases
* Non-invertible symmetry sectors

Outside lattice calculations and a few supersymmetric examples, there exists no general formalism for determining low-energy Wilson coefficients non-perturbatively.

This white paper develops a new framework:

# Non-Perturbative Wilsonian Reconstruction (NPWR)

which replaces operator power counting by a dynamical geometric map between UV operator algebras and IR EFT data.

The central claim is:

> Wilson coefficients are emergent coordinates on a manifold generated by coarse-grained correlation functions, operator entanglement, and RG transport.

The EFT landscape becomes a dynamical geometric object rather than a finite perturbative expansion.
