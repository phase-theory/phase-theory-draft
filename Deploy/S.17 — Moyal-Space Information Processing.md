A Framework for Quantum Information Processing in Moyal Geometry

Abstract

The Moyal plane represents one of the most extensively studied realizations of non-commutative geometry. In this framework, spatial coordinates cease to commute and are instead promoted to operators obeying nontrivial commutation relations. Ordinary multiplication of functions is replaced by the Moyal star product, fundamentally altering the geometric and informational structure of space.

While Moyal geometry has been investigated in quantum field theory, deformation quantization, condensed matter systems, and quantum gravity models, its implications for quantum information science remain largely unexplored. Existing studies focus primarily on mathematical structure rather than information processing capabilities.

This paper develops the theory of Moyal-Space Information Processing (MSIP), establishing a complete framework for computation, communication, storage, measurement, error correction, and algorithmic dynamics within non-commutative geometric spaces. We define Moyal information states, star-product logic, non-local geometric computation, Moyal quantum channels, deformation-assisted error protection, spectral information flow, and candidate physical realizations. The resulting framework extends quantum information theory beyond classical geometry and establishes Moyal space as a potential computational substrate.

Conclusions

Moyal-Space Information Processing extends quantum information theory into non-commutative geometric environments governed by the Moyal star product. By encoding information into coordinate algebras, spectral sectors, deformation structures, and non-local geometric relationships, computation becomes an intrinsic property of geometry itself.

The framework introduces Moyal information units, star-product logic, deformation encoding, Moyal quantum channels, spectral communication, star-stabilizer codes, deformation-protected memories, and fault-tolerant non-commutative computation. It provides the first comprehensive information-theoretic treatment of Moyal geometry as a computational substrate.

Within the Structural Sector, Moyal-Space Information Processing serves as the operational counterpart to Non-Commutative Geometric Qubits. Together they establish a complete foundation for information processing on quantum geometry and open new directions connecting quantum information science, non-commutative geometry, condensed matter analogues, and quantum gravity.
