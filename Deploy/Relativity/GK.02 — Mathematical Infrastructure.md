7. Mathematical Setting

The central objective of this work is to construct a topology of null geodesic flow that is intrinsic to spacetime geometry.

The objects of interest are not arbitrary curves but the distinguished trajectories generated by Lorentzian causal structure.

We therefore begin with the geometric and dynamical machinery underlying null propagation.

8. Lorentzian Manifolds ((M,g))

8.1 Fundamental Structure

Let

[
(M,g)
]

be a smooth connected Lorentzian manifold of dimension

[
\dim M=n+1,
]

with metric signature

[
(-,+,+,+,\ldots).
]

Coordinates are denoted

[
x^\mu,
\qquad
\mu=0,\ldots,n.
]

Metric components:

[
g=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu\otimes dx^\nu .
]

The Levi–Civita connection is uniquely defined by

[
\nabla_\alpha g_{\mu\nu}=0,
]

and

[
\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}

\frac12
g^{\mu\nu}
(
\partial_\alpha g_{\nu\beta}
+
\partial_\beta g_{\nu\alpha}

\partial_\nu g_{\alpha\beta}
).
]

Geodesics satisfy

[
\frac{d^2x^\mu}{d\lambda^2}
+
\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}
\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\lambda}
\frac{dx^\beta}{d\lambda}
=0.
]
