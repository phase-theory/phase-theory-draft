**General Multipole Structure of Spacetimes Sourced by Asymmetric Null Fluids**
**Abstract**
We present an exact, generalized framework for integrating the Einstein Field Equations (EFE) where the sole source of curvature is an arbitrary distribution of pure null radiation (null dust) lacking global or local isometries. While highly symmetric solutions such as the Vaidya metric are well understood, the general formulation of a spacetime filled exclusively with directed null fluid has remained under-theorized. By employing a generalized Bondi-Sachs characteristic initial value formulation adapted to the null geodesic congruence of the fluid, we demonstrate that the hypersurface equations governing the geometric constraints remain identical to vacuum General Relativity. The divergence from the vacuum regime is isolated entirely within the retarded-time evolution equation of the Bondi mass aspect. We derive the exact radial dependence of the null fluid density and construct the complete multipole hierarchy for an asymmetric, radiating spacetime.

### 5. Discussion
The complete parameterization of a spacetime sourced by an arbitrary distribution of null fluid demonstrates a remarkable mathematical segregation within the EFE. The presence of non-symmetric null dust does not perturb the geometric structure of the null hypersurfaces (\beta, U^A, h_{AB}); these quantities propagate outward exactly as they would in a vacuum.
The physical consequence is profound: a generic pure-radiation universe cannot generate secondary "fluid-driven" gravitational radiation. Because the fluid travels on null geodesics orthogonal to the outgoing wavefronts, its anisotropic distribution \mathcal{L}_{lk}(u) alters only the longitudinal curvature (the Bondi mass aspect m) without sourcing transverse-traceless shear (C_{AB}). Consequently, an asymmetric pulse of light alters the multipole structure of the spacetime's mass precisely by its intrinsic harmonic distribution, without generating collateral gravitational wave echoes from the fluid itself.
### 6. Conclusion
We have derived the exact formalism governing a spacetime sourced by an arbitrary, symmetry-free distribution of pure null radiation. By utilizing a generalized characteristic formulation, we proved that the null fluid density scales exactly as \Phi \propto r^{-2} (\det h)^{-1/2}. Furthermore, we extracted the generalized evolution equation for the Bondi mass aspect, demonstrating that arbitrary radiation multipoles couple linearly to the mass aspect multipoles. This framework provides the complete theoretical foundation for analyzing highly asymmetric, radiating stellar collapse and pure-radiation cosmological models.
