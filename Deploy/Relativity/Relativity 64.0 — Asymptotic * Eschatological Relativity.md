## Abstract

Asymptotic / Eschatological Relativity is the hypothesis that the ultimate future functions as an observational frame. Heat death, cosmic horizons, black-hole evaporation, vacuum decay, computational limits, and the long-term fate of intelligence condition what can count as durable knowledge, stable value, and civilizational survival. This framework does not require religious eschatology. It is the recognition that asymptotic boundary conditions shape present meaning. Let \(t\to\infty\) denote the far future. The asymptotic structure of a theory, civilization, or knowledge system may be written as

\[
\Theta_\infty
=
\lim_{t\to\infty}
\Theta(t),
\]

when such a limit exists. The central principle is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The future is a frame; ultimate limits relativize present significance.}
}
\]

What matters now is partly relative to what can persist, be remembered, remain invariant, or continue to matter under cosmic time. Thus long-term survival, knowledge preservation, ethical continuity, and scientific meaning become relativistic problems. Asymptotic / Eschatological Relativity integrates cosmology, thermodynamics, black-hole physics, information theory, existential risk, and long-term ethics into a single framework. It asserts that the far future is not merely a distant time. It is a frame through which the present is evaluated.

\approx

## 10. Computational Limits

Intelligence is physical.

Computation requires energy.

\[
\nu
\leq
\frac{2E}{\pi\hbar}
\]

elementary operations per second.

Landauer’s principle states that erasing one bit costs at least:

\[
E_{\text{erase}}
\geq
k_{\text{B}}T\ln 2.
\]

The Bekenstein bound limits information storage:

\[
I
\leq
\frac{2\pi E R}{\hbar c\ln 2}.
\]

In a universe with finite usable energy and finite horizon entropy, computation is bounded.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Intelligence is constrained by ultimate thermodynamics.}
}
