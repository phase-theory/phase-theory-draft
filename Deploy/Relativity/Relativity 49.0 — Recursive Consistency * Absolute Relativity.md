## Abstract

Recursive Consistency / Absolute Relativity is the closure of the relativistic sequence. It asserts that the final invariant is not a theory, not a substance, not a law, and not a description. It is the self-consistent fixed point of all possible admissible relativizations. If every structure can be relativized — motion, geometry, frames, locality, spacetime, description, modality, logic, mathematical structure, substance, substrate, adjacency, knowability, phase, agency — then the only remaining absolute is the requirement of consistency under all admissible transformations, including transformations of that requirement itself. Let \(\mathcal{R}\) denote the operation of relativization. The final structure is a fixed point,

\[
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{R}^*)
=
\mathcal{R}^*.
\]

The central principle is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The absolute is the invariant consistency of all self-consistent relativizations.}
}
\]

This is not a return to pre-critical absolutism. It is the discovery that the only non-relativizable remainder is recursive consistency itself. The absolute is not an exception to relativity. It is the fixed point of relativity. This framework unifies gauge invariance, diffeomorphism invariance, duality, categorical equivalence, logical relativity, semantic relativity, modal relativity, mathematical-structure relativity, and meta-relativity into a single closure principle. It avoids performative contradiction by treating the principle of relativity not as a privileged description but as an idempotent operation whose self-application is stable.
