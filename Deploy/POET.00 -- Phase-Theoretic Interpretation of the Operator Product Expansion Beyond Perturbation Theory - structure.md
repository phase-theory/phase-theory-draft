### Central Concept

The original NOET framework proposed:

[
\text{OPE}
==========

\text{Spectral Decomposition}.
]

The Phase-Theoretic extension proposes:

[
\boxed{
\text{OPE}
==========

\text{Local Projection Between Quantum Phases}
}
]

where local operator products do not merely probe spectra but probe the **phase geometry of the quantum vacuum itself**.

In this framework:

[
\mathcal O_A(x)\mathcal O_B(0)
]

is interpreted as a transition between nearby points on a phase manifold

[
\mathcal M_{\text{phase}},
]

and the OPE coefficients become geometric transition amplitudes on that manifold:

[
C_{AB}^{;;C}(x)
===============

\mathcal P_{AB}^{;;C}
\exp
!\left(
-\int_\gamma \Gamma
\right),
]

with (\Gamma) a phase connection and (\gamma) a path through local phase space.

This turns the OPE into a theory of:

* vacuum geometry,
* phase transitions,
* confinement phases,
* topological sectors,
* information flow,

rather than solely a short-distance expansion. It is therefore a genuine successor framework to NOET, which could be called **Phase Operator Expansion Theory (POET)**.
