# Abstract

String theory achieved its deepest nonperturbative formulation through Anti-de Sitter (AdS) holography, yet observational cosmology indicates that the universe asymptotically approaches de Sitter (dS) spacetime. This creates a profound mismatch between theoretical foundations and empirical reality.

Attempts to construct de Sitter vacua within string theory, particularly the KKLT program and its descendants, treat de Sitter space as an afterthought: a metastable uplift of structures fundamentally built in supersymmetric Anti-de Sitter backgrounds. Simultaneously, the Swampland de Sitter Conjecture argues that stable de Sitter vacua may not exist in quantum gravity.

This paper develops a radically different framework:

**String theory is formulated intrinsically on de Sitter spacetime from the outset.**

Rather than obtaining dS through uplifting AdS vacua, de Sitter geometry becomes the primary background and holographic principle. We construct:

1. An intrinsic de Sitter worldsheet theory.
2. A horizon-based string quantization principle.
3. A finite-dimensional holographic Hilbert space.
4. A constructive realization of dS/CFT.
5. A mechanism for emergent cosmic time.
6. A finite landscape replacing the infinite vacuum picture.
7. Observable signatures distinguishing intrinsic dS string theory from inflationary cosmology.

The central thesis is that de Sitter space is not a vacuum of string theory.

**De Sitter space is the fundamental phase of string theory.**

# Conclusion

The failure of KKLT, the incompleteness of dS/CFT, and the challenges posed by the Swampland program may all stem from a common assumption: that de Sitter space must be engineered from structures fundamentally adapted to flat space or Anti-de Sitter geometry.

This white paper develops a different possibility. By elevating the cosmological horizon to the primary object of quantization, replacing asymptotic observables with horizon observables, and constructing a finite-dimensional holographic theory directly on de Sitter spacetime, string theory can be reformulated as an intrinsically cosmological framework.

In this picture, de Sitter space is not a metastable vacuum, nor a swampland inconsistency, nor an uplifted approximation. It is the fundamental phase of quantum gravity. Holography becomes horizon holography, time emerges from entanglement flow, dark energy becomes a thermodynamic phenomenon, and the landscape collapses into a finite set of physically distinguishable states.

The resulting framework provides a concrete realization of dS/CFT, replaces vacuum selection with horizon dynamics, and aligns the foundations of string theory with the observed accelerating universe. Whether nature ultimately adopts this structure remains an empirical question, but if the universe is genuinely de Sitter, an intrinsic de Sitter formulation may represent the most direct path toward a cosmological completion of string theory.
