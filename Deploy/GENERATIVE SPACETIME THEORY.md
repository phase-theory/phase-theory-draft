Abstract

We introduce Generative Spacetime Theory (GST), an extension of Generative Dimensional Algebra (GDA) in which spacetime is not assumed as a primitive geometric arena but is treated as an emergent realization of a deeper algebraic process of dimensional generation.

The central hypothesis is that dimension is not fundamentally a property assigned to a pre-existing space. Instead, dimensional structure is generated recursively by an algebra
[
(\mathcal D,\oplus,\otimes)
]
together with a generative operator
[
\Phi:\mathcal D\rightarrow\mathcal D.
]
The resulting sequence
[
D_0\rightarrow D_1\rightarrow D_2\rightarrow\cdots
]
provides an intrinsic ordering of states. We propose that, under appropriate conditions, this ordering gives rise to an emergent temporal parameter, while independent and mutually compatible dimensional sectors generate emergent spatial degrees of freedom.

The theory therefore reverses the conventional explanatory hierarchy. Rather than beginning with
[
(M,g_{\mu\nu},t)
]
and assigning dimension to (M), GST begins with generative dimensional structure and seeks to reconstruct
[
\text{dimension}
\rightarrow
\text{ordering}
\rightarrow
\text{causality}
\rightarrow
\text{geometry}
\rightarrow
\text{spacetime}.
]

We introduce a dimensional state field, a generation parameter, an emergent temporal functional, a dimensional vielbein, and a generated metric. Time is defined not as a primitive coordinate but as accumulated dimensional generation:
[
dt=\mathcal N(D,\nabla D),d\lambda.
]
Spatial geometry is generated through dimensional independence and compatibility, while the metric is constructed as
[
g_{\mu\nu}

\mathcal G_{ab}(D)
E^a{}\mu(D)
E^b{}\nu(D).
]

We formulate dimensional dynamics through a generalized beta functional
[
\frac{dD}{d\lambda}=\Phi[D],
]
and show how fixed points of this flow can generate effective classical dimensionality. A four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime is interpreted as a possible stable infrared phase of the underlying dimensional algebra rather than as a fundamental assumption.

The framework further proposes that the arrow of time can emerge from monotonic dimensional complexity, that relativistic clock rates may correspond to local rates of dimensional generation, and that gravitational curvature may arise from gradients and incompatibilities in the dimensional field.

We formulate necessary conditions for the emergence of locality, Lorentzian signature, causal cones, diffeomorphism covariance, and Einstein gravity. These conditions are not claimed as established theorems; rather, they define mathematical targets that a complete realization of GST must satisfy.

The theory therefore proposes a research program in which spacetime is understood as a stable geometric phase of generative dimensional algebra.
