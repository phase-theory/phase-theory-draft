# 8. Recursive Quantization Theory

## 8.1 Introduction

The central challenge of finite-volume many-body quantum field theory is the derivation of exact quantization conditions valid for arbitrary particle number.

For two particles, the spectrum is governed by the Lüscher relation.

For three particles, the spectrum is determined by the Hansen–Sharpe quantization condition.

The objective of this section is to establish a recursive framework from which all finite-volume quantization conditions emerge as special cases.

---

## 8.2 Quantization as a Spectral Problem

Let

[
H_N
]

denote the exact finite-volume (N)-body Hamiltonian.

The spectrum satisfies

[
H_N \Psi_n
==========

E_n \Psi_n.
]

Equivalently,

[
\det(E-H_N)=0.
]

All quantization conditions arise as realizations of this spectral identity.

---

The Hamiltonian admits a cluster decomposition:

[

\Delta H_N.
]

The operator

[

]

Consequently,

[

===

H_1
+
\sum_{k=2}^{N}
\Delta H_k.
]

## 8.4 Recursive Resolvent Identity

Define

[

]

[

## 8.9 Recursive Quantization Principle

The finite-volume (N)-body spectrum is generated recursively from the ((N-1))-body spectrum through the addition of a compact-space interaction operator.
