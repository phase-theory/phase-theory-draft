Abstract

We introduce Phase-Theoretic Phase Space (PTPS), the natural state space of Φ-Wave Theory. Unlike the phase space of classical mechanics, whose coordinates consist of particle positions and momenta, PTPS is the infinite-dimensional manifold of globally admissible phase configurations. Every physically realizable state of the universe corresponds to a point in this space, while physical evolution is represented as continuous trajectories through admissible phase configurations.

Within this framework, particles, fields, spacetime geometry, quantum states, and observers are not independent coordinates but emergent structures encoded within the geometry of the underlying phase field. Quantum mechanics becomes statistical motion on PTPS, classical mechanics appears as geodesic flow within admissible sectors, and topology partitions PTPS into disconnected regions corresponding to conserved particle sectors. The resulting formulation unifies classical mechanics, quantum theory, topology, and emergent spacetime within a single configuration geometry.
