Abstract

We develop a phase-geometric mechanism for anomaly cancellation in Φ-Wave Theory, showing that gauge and gravitational anomalies are not independently imposed but instead emerge as consequences of the global Admissibility Principle. Chiral fermions arising from topological phase defects contribute anomaly terms determined by their gauge representations, topological indices, and localization structure. We show that only globally admissible collections of defect sectors survive quantization, and that admissibility eliminates anomaly-producing matter spectra. This provides a geometric and topological explanation for the anomaly-free structure of the Standard Model as an allowed sector of Φ-Wave Theory.
