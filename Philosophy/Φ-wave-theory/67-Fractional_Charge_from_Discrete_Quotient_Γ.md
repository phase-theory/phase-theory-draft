Abstract

We develop the origin of fractional electric charge in Φ-Wave Theory by showing that the physical gauge structure is not simply SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1), but rather a quotient by a discrete subgroup:

As a result, topological defects may carry fractional effective electric charge while preserving global phase admissibility. Quark charges arise naturally as quotient-restricted topological sectors of the unified phase bundle.
