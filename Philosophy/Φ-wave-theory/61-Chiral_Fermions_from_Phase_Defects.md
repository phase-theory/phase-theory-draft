Topological Zero Modes, Index Structure, and Emergent Matter

Abstract

We develop a mechanism by which chiral fermions emerge from topological defects in Φ-Wave Theory. Using the defect topology established in earlier papers and the gauge-sector embedding of the Standard Model, we show that localized zero modes of a Dirac-type operator on nontrivial phase backgrounds naturally generate chiral matter sectors. The chirality, multiplicity, and gauge representations of these zero modes are determined by defect topology, bundle structure, and admissibility constraints. We derive the localization conditions, connect chirality to index theory, and show how left-handed weak doublets and right-handed singlets can arise as distinct defect-bound sectors. This provides a candidate origin for quarks and leptons as emergent topological excitations of phase geometry.
