Abstract

We develop the charge lattice structure of Φ-Wave Theory, showing that electric charge sectors form a discrete lattice determined by the topology of the electromagnetic phase bundle, the discrete quotient structure of the unified gauge group, and global admissibility constraints. The first Chern class determines the fundamental charge unit, while quotient topology generates fractional sublattices. Matter fields occupy discrete lattice sites corresponding to topologically allowed electromagnetic sectors. This framework explains integer charge, quark fractional charge, charge conservation, and the exclusion of non-admissible charge values.
