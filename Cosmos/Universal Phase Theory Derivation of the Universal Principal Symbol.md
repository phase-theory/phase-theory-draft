Abstract

The previous formulation of the Phase Equivalence Principle identified the decisive condition

The universal principal symbol therefore becomes a decisive UPT gate. Its derivation would establish a deep structural reason for universal propagation. Its failure would produce a precise route to equivalence-principle violation.
