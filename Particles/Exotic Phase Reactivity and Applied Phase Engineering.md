## 1. The Reactivity Boundary in UPT

Standard chemical reactivity requires three conditions:
1.  **Mass scale:** Particle mass must be $\lesssim$ GeV to allow atomic orbitals ($a_0 \propto 1/m$) to extend beyond the nucleus.
2.  **Stability:** The particle must survive longer than molecular vibrational timescales ($\sim 10^{-12}$ s).
3.  **Quantum Statistics:** The particle must possess a Hilbert space and obey the Pauli Exclusion Principle to form molecular bonds.

As established in UPT, particles are stable phase-sector orbits $\mathfrak{P}_q = [\Phi_q]_{\mathscr{G}_\Phi}$. The undiscovered strata of UPT generally fail one or more of the above conditions, rendering them "chemically dark." The **Exceptions** are specific topological or geometric phase sectors that, while failing standard chemistry, couple to SM matter through **catastrophic topological relaxation, extreme electromagnetic field gradients, or universal phase susceptibility ($\boldsymbol{\chi}_\Phi$)**.
