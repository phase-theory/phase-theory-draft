## Abstract

We derive a new physical framework from Recursive Algebraic Topology (RAT). Starting from the RAT tower associated with spacetime and internal gauge configuration spaces, we construct **Recursive Field Theory** (RFT), in which physical fields are not confined to a single cohomological level but form a hierarchy of recursive gauge and geometric potentials. The recursive operator
\[
\mathcal R(A)=H^\bullet\!\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_k(BA,k)\bigr)
\]
generates, at each level, new algebraic operations that appear physically as higher interactions, higher gauge curvatures, secondary topological charges, and recursive corrections to Einstein gravity. We derive recursive Yang–Mills equations,
\[
D^{[n]\mu}F^{[n]}_{\mu\nu}
+
\gamma_n\,\rho_n^{*}\!\left(D^{[n+1]\mu}F^{[n+1]}_{\mu\nu}\right)
=
J^{[n]}_\nu,
\]
and recursive Cartan gravitational equations,
\[
\varepsilon_{abcd}\,e^{[0]b}\wedge
\left(
R^{[0]cd}-\frac{\Lambda}{3}e^{[0]c}\wedge e^{[0]d}
\right)
=
8\pi G\,\ast\!\left(T^{[0]}_a+T^{\mathrm{rec}}_a\right),
\]
where \(T^{\mathrm{rec}}_a\) is an effective stress-energy current induced by higher recursive levels. We show that the RAT spectral sequence organizes quantum corrections: the \(E_1\)-page gives ordinary perturbative amplitudes, while higher differentials are Massey-type interactions producing selection rules, anomaly obstructions, and topological phase transitions. The theory predicts recursive topological charges, a tower of \(\theta\)-vacua, recursive corrections to black-hole entropy, and new topologically protected phases in quantum matter.

**Keywords:** recursive topology, \(A_\infty\)-algebras, recursive gauge theory, recursive gravity, Massey products, topological quantum field theory, higher structures.

## 1. Physical Principle

Classical field theory assigns to a spacetime manifold \(M\) a space of fields, usually sections of bundles over \(M\), and then constructs local differential operators, action functionals, and path integrals. Recursive Algebraic Topology suggests a deeper assignment:

> **Recursive Covariance Principle.**  
> The physical degrees of freedom associated with a space \(M\) are not exhausted by ordinary fields on \(M\). They are the full recursive tower generated by the cochain-level algebraic topology of \(M\) and of the relevant configuration spaces.

Let \(M\) be a \(d\)-dimensional oriented pseudo-Riemannian manifold. Let \(\Omega^\bullet(M)\) be its de Rham dg algebra, or more generally let \(C^\bullet(M;k)\) be a dg algebra of cochains. By homotopy transfer one obtains a minimal \(A_\infty\)-model
\[
A_0(M)
\simeq
\Omega^\bullet(M)
\]
with
\[
m_1=0,\qquad m_2=\wedge,\qquad m_3,m_4,\ldots
\]
The higher operations encode coherent failures of strict commutativity and associativity, i.e. Massey-type data.

Define recursively
\[
A_{n+1}(M)=\mathcal R(A_n(M))
=
H^\bullet\!\left(\operatorname{Hom}_k(BA_n(M),k)\right).
\]
The resulting tower
\[
A_0(M)\longrightarrow A_1(M)\longrightarrow A_2(M)\longrightarrow\cdots
\]
is the algebraic backbone of the physical theory.

We interpret the levels as follows:

| Recursive level | Physical interpretation |
|---:|---|
| \(n=0\) | ordinary fields, ordinary cohomology, classical gauge potentials |
| \(n=1\) | loop-space or stringy corrections, secondary gauge data |
| \(n=2\) | higher Massey interactions, persistent topological memory |
| \(n\ge 3\) | iterated loop-space, higher-brane and quantum-topological corrections |

The central physical claim is that the higher levels are not auxiliary mathematical decorations. They generate new dynamical terms, new conserved charges, and new selection rules.

## 2. Recursive Differential Geometry

### 2.1 Recursive form algebra

For each recursive level \(n\), let \(A_n\) be the minimal \(A_\infty\)-algebra associated with \(M\). We form the recursive form complex
\[
\Omega^\bullet_R(M)
=
\bigoplus_{n\ge 0}
\Omega^\bullet(M)\otimes V_n,
\]
where \(V_n\) is the graded internal vector space underlying \(A_n\), shifted appropriately for gauge-theoretic use. The ordinary exterior derivative \(d\) acts on \(\Omega^\bullet(M)\), while the \(A_\infty\)-operations act internally.

A **recursive connection** is a degree-one element
\[
\mathcal A
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}\mathcal A^{[n]},
\qquad
\mathcal A^{[n]}\in \Omega^1(M)\otimes V_n.
\]
The component \(\mathcal A^{[0]}\) is the ordinary gauge potential; the higher components \(\mathcal A^{[n]}\), \(n\ge 1\), are recursive gauge potentials.

Let
\[
\rho_n:V_n\longrightarrow V_{n+1}
\]
denote the recursive lift induced by the bar-dual construction and the chosen homotopy-transfer data. Physically, \(\rho_n\) embeds lower-level curvature data into the next recursive level.

### 2.2 Recursive curvature

Define the **recursive curvature**
\[
\mathcal F
=
\mathfrak D\mathcal A
+
\frac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_R,
\]
where \(\mathfrak D\) is the recursive differential and \([\cdot,\cdot]_R\) is the recursive graded bracket induced by the convolution algebra and the higher \(A_\infty\)-operations.

Levelwise, the curvature takes the form
\[
\boxed{
F^{[n]}
=
dA^{[n]}
+
\frac12[A^{[n]},A^{[n]}]_n
+
\sum_{r\ge 3}m_r^{[n]}
\left(A^{[n]},\ldots,A^{[n]}\right)
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(F^{[n-1]}\right)
}
\]
for \(n\ge 1\), while
\[
F^{[0]}
=
dA^{[0]}
+
\frac12[A^{[0]},A^{[0]}]_0.
\]
Here \(\lambda_n\) are recursive coupling constants.

In local coordinates, writing
\[
A^{[n]}
=
A^{[n]a}_\mu T_a\,dx^\mu,
\]
the recursive curvature components are
\[
\boxed{
F^{[n]a}_{\mu\nu}
=
\partial_\mu A^{[n]a}_\nu
-
\partial_\nu A^{[n]a}_\mu
+
f^a{}_{bc}A^{[n]b}_\mu A^{[n]c}_\nu
+
M^{[n]a}_{\mu\nu}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(F^{[n-1]a}_{\mu\nu}\right)
}
\]
where
\[
M^{[n]}
=
\sum_{r\ge 3}m_r^{[n]}
\left(A^{[n]},\ldots,A^{[n]}\right)
\]
collects the higher \(A_\infty\)-corrections.

### 2.3 Recursive gauge transformations

Let
\[
\Lambda
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}\Lambda^{[n]}
\]
be a recursive gauge parameter, with \(\Lambda^{[n]}\in\Omega^0(M)\otimes V_n\). The recursive gauge variation is
\[
\delta_\Lambda \mathcal A
=
\mathfrak D\Lambda
+
[\mathcal A,\Lambda]_R.
\]
Levelwise,
\[
\delta_{\Lambda^{[n]}}A^{[n]}
=
d\Lambda^{[n]}
+
[A^{[n]},\Lambda^{[n]}]_n
+
\sum_{r\ge 3}
\sum_{j=1}^r
m_r^{[n]}
\left(
A^{[n]},\ldots,\Lambda^{[n]},\ldots,A^{[n]}
\right)
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(\delta_{\Lambda^{[n-1]}}A^{[n-1]}\right).
\]

The curvature transforms covariantly:
\[
\delta_\Lambda F^{[n]}
=
[F^{[n]},\Lambda^{[n]}]_n
+
\text{higher coherent terms}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(\delta_{\Lambda^{[n-1]}}F^{[n-1]}\right).
\]

### 2.4 Recursive Bianchi identity

The \(A_\infty\)-identities imply
\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak D_{\mathcal A}\mathcal F=0.
}
\]
Levelwise this becomes
\[
D^{[n]}F^{[n]}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(D^{[n-1]}F^{[n-1]}\right)
+
N^{[n]}
=
0,
\]
where \(D^{[n]}\) is the recursive covariant derivative at level \(n\), and \(N^{[n]}\) denotes higher coherent corrections determined by the Stasheff identities.

In tensor notation,
\[
\boxed{
D^{[n]}_{[\mu}F^{[n]}_{\nu\rho]}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(
D^{[n-1]}_{[\mu}F^{[n-1]}_{\nu\rho]}
\right)
+
N^{[n]}_{\mu\nu\rho}
=
0.
}
\]
This is the recursive Bianchi identity. It reduces to the ordinary Bianchi identity when all higher recursive levels vanish.

## 3. Recursive Yang–Mills Theory

### 3.1 Recursive action

Let \(M\) carry a metric \(g_{\mu\nu}\) and Hodge star \(\ast\). Assume each internal recursive level carries a nondegenerate invariant pairing
\[
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_n.
\]
Define the recursive Yang–Mills action
\[
\boxed{
S_{\mathrm{RYM}}
=
-\frac12
\sum_{n\ge 0}
\frac{1}{g_n^2}
\int_M
\left\langle
F^{[n]}\wedge \ast F^{[n]}
\right\rangle_n
+
S_{\mathrm{top}}.
}
\]
The topological part is
\[
S_{\mathrm{top}}
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}
\theta_n\,Q^{[n]},
\]
where \(Q^{[n]}\) are recursive characteristic numbers discussed below.

The level \(n=0\) term is the ordinary Yang–Mills action. The higher terms introduce new kinetic and topological structures generated by the RAT tower.

### 3.2 Variation of the recursive curvature

Under a variation \(\delta A^{[n]}\), the recursive curvature varies as
\[
\delta F^{[n]}
=
D^{[n]}\delta A^{[n]}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(\delta F^{[n-1]}\right)
+
\text{higher coherent terms}.
\]
For cyclic \(A_\infty\)-structures, the higher coherent terms can be absorbed into the definition of \(D^{[n]}\). We therefore write schematically
\[
\delta F^{[n]}
=
D^{[n]}\delta A^{[n]}
+
\lambda_n\,\rho_{n-1}\!\left(D^{[n-1]}\delta A^{[n-1]}\right).
\]

### 3.3 Derivation of the recursive field equations

The variation of the action is
\[
\delta S_{\mathrm{RYM}}
=
-\sum_{n\ge 0}
\frac{1}{g_n^2}
\int_M
\left\langle
\delta F^{[n]},\ast F^{[n]}
\right\rangle_n.
\]
Substituting the variation of \(F^{[n]}\),
\[
\delta S_{\mathrm{RYM}}
=
-\sum_{n\ge 0}
\frac{1}{g_n^2}
\int_M
\left\langle
D^{[n]}\delta A^{[n]},\ast F^{[n]}
\right\rangle_n
-
\sum_{n\ge 1}
\frac{\lambda_n}{g_n^2}
\int_M
\left\langle
\rho_{n-1}\!\left(D^{[n-1]}\delta A^{[n-1]}\right),
\ast F^{[n]}
\right\rangle_n.
\]
Using the adjoint operators \(D^{[n]*}\) and \(\rho_n^*\), defined by
\[
\langle D^{[n]}\alpha,\beta\rangle
=
\langle \alpha,D^{[n]*}\beta\rangle,
\qquad
\langle \rho_n\alpha,\beta\rangle
=
\langle \alpha,\rho_n^*\beta\rangle,
\]
we collect the coefficient of \(\delta A^{[n]}\). The result is
\[
\delta S_{\mathrm{RYM}}
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}
\int_M
\left\langle
\delta A^{[n]},
\frac{1}{g_n^2}D^{[n]*}\ast F^{[n]}
+
\frac{\lambda_{n+1}}{g_{n+1}^2}
\rho_n^*D^{[n+1]*}\ast F^{[n+1]}
\right\rangle_n.
\]
In the presence of matter currents \(J^{[n]}\), stationarity gives
\[
\boxed{
D^{[n]*}\ast F^{[n]}
+
\gamma_n\,\rho_n^*\!\left(
D^{[n+1]*}\ast F^{[n+1]}
\right)
=
g_n^2 J^{[n]},
}
\]
with
\[
\gamma_n
=
\frac{g_n^2\lambda_{n+1}}{g_{n+1}^2}.
\]

In components,
\[
\boxed{
D^{[n]\mu}F^{[n]a}_{\mu\nu}
+
\gamma_n\,\rho_n^*
\left(
D^{[n+1]\mu}F^{[n+1]a}_{\mu\nu}
\right)
=
J^{[n]a}_\nu.
}
\]
This is the **recursive Yang–Mills equation**.

### 3.4 Physical meaning: higher topology as a source

The new term
\[
\gamma_n\,\rho_n^*
\left(
D^{[n+1]*}\ast F^{[n+1]}
\right)
\]
means that the next recursive level acts as an effective current for the lower-level gauge field. Thus even if ordinary matter is absent,
\[
J^{[n]}=0,
\]
a nontrivial higher recursive curvature can source the ordinary gauge field.

We state this as a theorem.

**Theorem 3.1: Topological source theorem.**  
Let \(M\) be compact and suppose the recursive curvature \(F^{[n+1]}\) represents a nonzero recursive cohomology class whose image under \(\rho_n^*\) is nonvanishing. Then the level-\(n\) recursive Yang–Mills equation admits no solution with \(F^{[n]}=0\) unless
\[
\rho_n^*\left[D^{[n+1]*}\ast F^{[n+1]}\right]=0.
\]
Thus higher RAT classes generate physical gauge fields.

*Proof.* If \(F^{[n]}=0\), the recursive equation reduces to
\[
\gamma_n\rho_n^*
\left(
D^{[n+1]*}\ast F^{[n+1]}
\right)
=
0.
\]
If the recursive class is nonzero and has nonzero projection, this equation is violated. Hence the lower-level field must become nonzero. \(\square\)

This is a genuinely new physical effect: topology at higher recursive levels becomes a dynamical source for lower-level gauge curvature.

## 4. Recursive Topological Charges and \(\theta\)-Vacua

### 4.1 Recursive Chern characters

For a recursive curvature \(F^{[n]}\), define the recursive Chern character forms
\[
\operatorname{ch}^{[n]}_k
=
\frac{1}{k!}
\operatorname{Tr}\left(
F^{[n]}
\right)^k.
\]
Because of the recursive Bianchi identity, these forms are closed up to recursive exact corrections:
\[
d\,\operatorname{ch}^{[n]}_k
=
\text{recursive transgression terms}.
\]
A fully closed recursive Chern character is obtained by summing over the tower:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ch}_R
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}
\operatorname{Tr}
\exp\left(
F^{[n]}
+
\lambda_n\rho_{n-1}F^{[n-1]}
\right).
}
\]
The recursive Bianchi identity implies
\[
d_R\operatorname{Ch}_R=0,
\]
where \(d_R\) is the recursive differential.

### 4.2 Recursive characteristic numbers

On a closed \(2k\)-manifold, define
\[
\boxed{
Q^{[n]}_k
=
\frac{1}{(2\pi)^k k!}
\int_M
\operatorname{Tr}\left(
F^{[n]}
\right)^k.
}
\]
More generally, mixed recursive charges exist:
\[
\boxed{
Q^{[n,n-1]}_k
=
\int_M
\operatorname{Tr}
\left[
\left(F^{[n]}\right)^{k-1}
\wedge
\rho_{n-1}\!\left(F^{[n-1]}\right)
\right].
}
\]
These charges are invariant under recursive gauge transformations and are quantized whenever the corresponding recursive characteristic classes are integral.

### 4.3 Recursive \(\theta\)-vacua

The topological action is
\[
S_{\mathrm{top}}
=
\sum_{n\ge 0}
\theta_n Q^{[n]}
+
\sum_{n\ge 1}
\widetilde\theta_n Q^{[n,n-1]}.
\]
Thus the vacuum structure is enriched from a single \(\theta\)-angle to a tower
\[
\{\theta_n,\widetilde\theta_n\}_{n\ge 0}.
\]
Instanton amplitudes become
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A
\sim
\sum_{\{q_n\}}
\exp\left[
-\sum_{n}
\frac{8\pi^2}{g_n^2}|q^{[n]}|
+
i\sum_n\theta_n q^{[n]}
+
i\sum_n\widetilde\theta_n q^{[n,n-1]}
\right].
}
\]
This predicts a hierarchy of topological sectors beyond ordinary Yang–Mills theory.

\wedge
\left(
F_t^{[n]}

Then

\boxed{

Thus recursive Chern–Simons forms transgress not merely primary characteristic classes but the entire recursive tower.

In three dimensions, the ordinary flatness equation \(F^{[0]}=0\) is therefore replaced by a recursive flatness condition. Nontrivial higher-level curvature forces lower-level fields to be non-flat.

### 5.3 Recursive anyons and effective levels

For a compact gauge group, the quantum theory depends on effective recursive levels

\boxed{

=

## 6. Recursive Gravity

### 6.1 Recursive Cartan geometry

Let \(M\) be a four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold. Introduce recursive vierbeine
\[
e^{[n]a}
=
e^{[n]a}_\mu dx^\mu

and recursive spin connections

\[
\boxed{
T^{[n]a}
=

+
\lambda_n\rho_{n-1}\!\left(T^{[n-1]a}\right).

Here \(M_R\) and \(M_T\) are higher \(A_\infty\)-corrections.

In components,
\[
R^{[n]ab}_{\mu\nu}

+

The physical spacetime metric is identified with the level-zero metric

The \(n=0\) term is the ordinary Einstein–Cartan action. Higher terms are recursive topological-geometric corrections.

The recursive stress tensor is defined by

=

The term \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{Massey}}\) contains higher-derivative corrections determined by the higher \(A_\infty\)-operations.

Thus higher recursive topology gravitates. Even in vacuum,
\[
T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{matter}}=0,
\]
nontrivial recursive topology can produce an effective geometric source:
\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}

+
\text{Massey corrections}.
\]
Thus ordinary torsionlessness is replaced by recursive torsion balance.

## 7. Recursive Quantum Theory

### 7.1 Recursive path integral

The recursive path integral is
\[
\boxed{
Z_R(M)
=
\sum_{\{q^{[n]}\}}
\int
\prod_{n\ge 0}
\mathcal D A^{[n]}
\mathcal D e^{[n]}
\,
\exp\left[
\frac{i}{\hbar}
S_R
+
i\sum_n\theta_n q^{[n]}
\right].
}
\]
The sum over \(\{q^{[n]}\}\) is over recursive topological sectors.

### 7.2 Spectral sequence of quantum corrections

The RAT spectral sequence governs the perturbative expansion. Recall the recursive spectral sequence:
\[
