# Descent Physics: Field Theory, Gravity, and Quantum Contextuality from Information Topos Theory

**Preprint**

## Abstract

We derive a physical theory from the axioms of Information Topos Theory (ITT). The central claim is that the primitive physical principle is not least action, quantization, or spacetime geometry taken as fundamental, but **informational descent**: local consistency glues to global structure precisely when descent holds. Physical systems are modeled as sheaves on information sites; physical states are global sections or, when global sections fail to exist, cohomologically structured families of local sections. We show that the continuum limit of a purely informational descent functional generates kinetic terms, gauge connections, curvature, and Einstein gravity. Quantum contextuality is reinterpreted as nontrivial sheaf cohomology, yielding a generalized Born rule and a corrected uncertainty relation containing an explicitly topological term. The resulting framework predicts additional geometric degrees of freedom—most naturally an antisymmetric descent two-form—coupled to gravity and sourced by contextual information. The paper provides formal derivations, tensorial field equations, and a program for experimental signatures.

## 1. Physical Postulates from Information Topos Theory

\[

A local physical configuration over \(\Gamma\) is a section

\[

\[

a family of local sections

\[

\[

\[

### Postulate 5 — Geometry as information distance

## 6. Quantum Theory as Nontrivial Descent

Quantum mechanics emerges when the information site has no global valuation.

\operatorname{Hom}(1,\Sigma) = \varnothing.

In ITT language, the spectral sheaf has no global section. Quantum contextuality is therefore the failure of global informational descent.
