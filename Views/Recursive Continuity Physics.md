We derive a physical theory from Recursive Continuity Theory II (RCT-II) by promoting recursive compatibility refinement from a topological construction to a physical ontology. We show that local relativistic field theory emerges in the separated dyadic limit of a recursive defect action; causal resolution filtrations reconstruct Lorentzian geometry; curvature appears as the holonomy defect of recursive coordinate compatibility; and Einstein gravity follows from an Einstein–Cartan defect functional. ## 1. Introduction: From Recursive Continuity to Physical Law

\[
E_0\supseteq E_1\supseteq E_2\supseteq\cdots
\]

\[
\mu_f(n+1)

> **Physical reality is a recursively generated compatibility structure. Fields, geometry, dynamics, and quantum behavior are consequences of the recursive elimination or stationary organization of compatibility defects.**

\[
E_0^M\supseteq E_1^M\supseteq E_2^M\supseteq\cdots,

\[

\]

\[
C_{n+1}^M\circ C_{n+1}^M\subseteq C_n^M.

The transitive closure of \(\bigcup_n C_n^M\) yields the causal order.

### Postulate III: Compatibility dynamics

\[

Observables are equivalence classes under unresolved compatibility. If

M^\sharp=M/E_\infty^M.
