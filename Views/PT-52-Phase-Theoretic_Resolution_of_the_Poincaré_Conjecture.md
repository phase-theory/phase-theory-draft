Abstract

The Poincaré Conjecture asserts that every closed, simply connected 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere S^3. This was rigorously proven via Ricci flow with surgery by Grigori Perelman, building on foundational work by Richard S. Hamilton.

This white paper reformulates the theorem within Phase Theory, where geometry and topology emerge from a single primitive: a globally constrained phase field. In this framework, manifolds are not purely geometric objects but phase-coherence containers, and topological classification reduces to the existence or absence of globally admissible phase winding.

We show that simply connected, compact 3-manifolds admit only one stable phase closure state: the 3-sphere.
