## Abstract

We derive a physical theory from Recursive Infinity Theory (RIT). The primitive datum is not a completed spacetime manifold, Hilbert space, or field algebra, but a physical growth presentation
\[
(\mathbf{Phys},\Gamma,\Sigma_0,\iota),
\]
where \(\Sigma_0\) is a finite pregeometric state, \(\Gamma\) is a local growth operator, and \(\iota:\mathrm{Id}\Rightarrow\Gamma\) is an inflation map. Spacetime is obtained as the directed colimit of an R-hierarchy
\[
\Sigma_{\alpha+1}=\Gamma(\Sigma_\alpha),\qquad
\Sigma_\lambda=\varinjlim_{\alpha<\lambda}\Sigma_\alpha .
\]
We show that the transition maps of such a hierarchy induce an emergent coframe, connection, curvature tensor, and metric. The RIT limit-closure theorem implies that physical law is the fixed-point condition of a continuous growth operator. Under locality, covariance, and finite-order differentiability, this fixed-point condition yields the Einstein equations with a cosmological constant, together with recursive scalar-tensor corrections. Gauge fields arise as holonomies of noncommuting directional growth operators; matter fields arise as labels of growing cells; quantum amplitudes arise as categorical traces over growth histories. The theory predicts: a bounce-type modification of the Friedmann equation from Cantorian saturation, logarithmic corrections to black-hole entropy, a running spectral dimension, and Planck-suppressed modified dispersion relations. The central claim is that physical law is the consistency condition for generated infinity.

## 1. Introduction

Classical theoretical physics assumes completed infinities at several levels. General relativity assumes a differentiable manifold with continuum cardinality. Quantum theory assumes Hilbert spaces, often infinite-dimensional. Quantum field theory assumes continuum spacetime and distributional fields. In each case, infinity is ontologically prior to dynamics: the arena exists, and the laws act within it.

Recursive Infinity Theory (RIT) proposes a reversal. Infinite structures are not primitive; they are generated by an explicit recursion
\[
\mathcal I_{n+1}=\Gamma(\mathcal I_n),
\]
extended transfinitely by colimits at limit stages. The fundamental object is not a completed infinity but a growth law.

In this paper we derive physics from that principle. We introduce **Recursive Physical Theory (RPT)**, in which spacetime, geometry, fields, and quantum amplitudes are generated by a physical growth operator. The central thesis is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Physical law is the fixed-point condition of a generative infinity.}
}
\]

More explicitly, we show that if \(\Sigma_\lambda\) is a limit stage of a physical R-hierarchy and \(\Gamma\) is continuous at \(\lambda\), then
\[
\Gamma(\Sigma_\lambda)\cong \Sigma_\lambda.
\]
This is the RIT limit-closure theorem translated into physics. Infinitesimally, it becomes a field equation. For a metric growth operator,
\[
\Gamma_\epsilon(g)_{\mu\nu}
=
g_{\mu\nu}
+
\epsilon\,E_{\mu\nu}[g,\psi]
+
O(\epsilon^2),
\]
the fixed-point condition gives
\[
E_{\mu\nu}[g,\psi]=0.
\]
Under locality, diffeomorphism covariance, and second-order differentiability, \(E_{\mu\nu}\) must be of Lovelock type. To lowest order one obtains
\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu},
\]
with recursive corrections determined by the growth tensor.

## 2. Physical Growth Presentations

### 2.1 The category of pregeometric states

Let \(\mathbf{Phys}\) be a category whose objects are finite or locally finite ranked physical structures. An object \(\Sigma\in\mathbf{Phys}\) is a tuple
\[
\Sigma=(K,\rho,\ell,\Phi),
\]
where:

1. \(K\) is a finite or locally finite combinatorial complex, causal complex, or cellular complex;
2. \(\rho:K\to\mathrm{Ord}\) is a rank function;
3. \(\ell\) assigns discrete metric data, such as edge lengths, volumes, or incidence weights;
4. \(\Phi\) assigns internal labels, such as phase factors, group elements, scalar labels, or spin labels.

A morphism
\[
f:\Sigma\to\Sigma'
\]
is a rank-preserving cellular embedding preserving metric and label data up to specified coherence.

The category \(\mathbf{Phys}\) is pregeometric. It does not yet contain a smooth manifold. Smooth spacetime will appear as a colimit.

### 2.2 Physical growth presentations

**Definition 2.1.** A *physical growth presentation* is a quadruple
\[
(\mathbf{Phys},\Gamma,\Sigma_0,\iota),
\]
where:

1. \(\mathbf{Phys}\) is the category of pregeometric states;
2. \(\Gamma:\mathbf{Phys}\to\mathbf{Phys}\) is an endofunctor, called the *physical growth operator*;
3. \(\Sigma_0\in\mathbf{Phys}\) is the initial state;
4. \(\iota:\mathrm{Id}_{\mathbf{Phys}}\Rightarrow\Gamma\) is a natural inflation map.

The associated physical R-hierarchy is defined by
\[
\Sigma_{\alpha+1}=\Gamma(\Sigma_\alpha),
\]
and for limit \(\lambda\),
\[
\Sigma_\lambda=\varinjlim_{\alpha<\lambda}\Sigma_\alpha.
\]

The maps
\[
i_{\alpha\beta}:\Sigma_\alpha\to\Sigma_\beta
\]
are the physical transition maps.

### 2.3 Physical admissibility axioms

We impose the following conditions on \(\Gamma\).

**Axiom 2.2 — Local finite generation.**  
For each \(\Sigma\), the extension \(\Gamma(\Sigma)\) is obtained by adding finitely or locally finitely many new cells, labels, and incidences according to local rules.

**Axiom 2.3 — Covariance.**  
For every automorphism \(\varphi:\Sigma\to\Sigma\),
\[
\Gamma(\varphi)\circ\iota_\Sigma
=
\iota_\Sigma\circ\varphi.
\]
Equivalently, growth does not depend on an external coordinate presentation.

**Axiom 2.4 — Inflationary inclusion.**  
Each component
\[
\iota_\Sigma:\Sigma\to\Gamma(\Sigma)
\]
is a monomorphism. The prior stage is retained as a substructure of the next stage.

**Axiom 2.5 — Cantorian non-collapse.**  
There is no physical morphism
\[
\Gamma(\Sigma)\to\Sigma
\]
that inverts the inflationary growth. This is the physical analogue of Cantorian strictness.

**Axiom 2.6 — Limit continuity.**  
For relevant limit ordinals \(\lambda\),
\[
\Gamma\left(\varinjlim_{\alpha<\lambda}\Sigma_\alpha\right)
\cong
\varinjlim_{\alpha<\lambda}\Gamma(\Sigma_\alpha).
\]

**Axiom 2.7 — Reflective physicality.**  
A continuum physical state is a limit stage \(\Sigma_\lambda\) that is closed under the generating law:
\[
\Gamma(\Sigma_\lambda)\cong\Sigma_\lambda.
\]

The last axiom is the physical translation of recursive closure. It replaces the idea of a pre-existing universe with the idea of a recursively stable limit.

### 2.4 Non-collapse and UV/IR separation

From the RIT non-collapse theorem, if \(\Gamma\) is Cantorian and \(\alpha<\beta\), then there is no morphism
\[
\Sigma_\beta\to\Sigma_\alpha
\]
compatible with the tower. Physically, this implies that higher-rank structure cannot be exactly compressed into lower-rank structure.

We interpret this as a structural UV/IR separation principle:

\[
\text{Ultraviolet generation cannot be exactly reduced to infrared data.}
\]

This does not forbid coarse-graining. It forbids exact collapse. Hence holographic descriptions are allowed as effective approximations but cannot be exact recursive reductions unless the growth operator itself is modified.

## 3. Emergent Geometry

### 3.1 Ranked complexes and discrete coframes

Let \(\Sigma_\alpha\) contain oriented edges \(e\). For each edge \(e\), define a discrete displacement vector
\[
\Delta x^\mu(e)
\]
relative to a local coordinate chart emerging at large rank. The inflation map induces refinements of edges into higher-rank paths.

Define a local coarse-graining window \(B_\alpha(x)\) around a point \(x\) in the emerging continuum. The discrete metric candidate is
\[
g^{(\alpha)}_{\mu\nu}(x)
=
\frac{1}{|B_\alpha(x)|}
\sum_{e\in B_\alpha(x)}
w_e\,
\Delta x_\mu(e)\Delta x_\nu(e),
\]
where \(w_e\) are positive weights determined by the growth measure.

If the directional distribution of edges becomes isotropic in the colimit, the limit
\[
g_{\mu\nu}(x)
=
\lim_{\alpha\to\lambda}g^{(\alpha)}_{\mu\nu}(x)
\]
exists and is a Lorentzian metric after analytic continuation or causal signature selection.

**Proposition 3.1.** Suppose the transition maps are asymptotically isotropic and the edge-weight measure has finite second moments. Then the colimit of the discrete two-point correlation defines a smooth pseudo-Riemannian metric.

*Proof sketch.* The transition maps preserve incidence and weights up to controlled corrections. Isotropy implies that the first anisotropic moments vanish in the limit. Finite second moments imply convergence of the covariance tensor. Smoothness follows from local finite generation and directed refinement. \(\square\)

Thus the metric is not postulated. It is the continuum covariance tensor of recursive growth.

### 3.2 Directional growth tensors

Let \(v^a\) be components of a vector in an internal frame. Under an infinitesimal transition from stage \(\alpha\) to \(\alpha+1\), write
\[

=
\left(
\delta^a{}_b
+
\epsilon\,\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}\,dx^\mu
\right)

+
O(\epsilon^2).
\]
Here
\[
\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}
\]
is the *directional growth tensor*. It measures how internal components are transported by one recursive step in direction \(\mu\).

Define the recursive covariant derivative
\[
\nabla_\mu v^a
=
\partial_\mu v^a
+
\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}v^b.
\]

The tensor \(\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}\) is not added by hand. It is the infinitesimal form of the transition maps \(i_{\alpha,\alpha+1}\).

### 3.3 Curvature from noncommuting growth

Consider two directional growth operations \(\Gamma_\mu\) and \(\Gamma_\nu\). Their commutator defines curvature. For a vector \(v^a\),
\[
[\nabla_\mu,\nabla_\nu]v^a
=
R^a{}_{b\mu\nu}v^b,
\]
with
\[
R^a{}_{b\mu\nu}
=
\partial_\mu\Gamma^a{}_{b\nu}
-
\partial_\nu\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}
+
\Gamma^a{}_{c\mu}\Gamma^c{}_{b\nu}
-
\Gamma^a{}_{c\nu}\Gamma^c{}_{b\mu}.
\]

Thus curvature is the failure of directional growth operations to commute.

The torsion tensor arises from the antisymmetric part of the growth of the coframe. Let \(e^a{}_\mu\) be the emergent coframe. Define
\[
T^a{}_{\mu\nu}
=
\partial_\mu e^a{}_\nu
-
\partial_\nu e^a{}_\mu
+
\Gamma^a{}_{b\mu}e^b{}_\nu
-
\Gamma^a{}_{b\nu}e^b{}_\mu.
\]

If the growth operator is symmetric under exchange of directional generators at low rank, then
\[
T^a{}_{\mu\nu}=0
\]
in the infrared. At high rank, antisymmetric growth produces torsion.

### 3.4 Bianchi identity from functoriality

Because \(\Gamma\) is a functor and \(\iota\) is natural, the transition maps satisfy cocycle compatibility. This implies the recursive Bianchi identity:
\[
\nabla_{[\lambda}R^a{}_{|b|\mu\nu]}=0.

Contracting gives
\[
\nabla^\mu G_{\mu\nu}=0,
\]
where
\[
G_{\mu\nu}
=
R_{\mu\nu}
-
\frac12 R g_{\mu\nu}.
\]

Therefore the geometric conservation law is a consequence of functorial recursion, not an independent physical postulate.

## 4. Recursive Dynamics and the Einstein Equation

\Gamma(\Sigma_\lambda)\cong\Sigma_\lambda.

E_{\mu\nu}[g,\psi]=0.

This is the fundamental dynamical equation of Recursive Physical Theory.

### 4.2 Locality and Lovelock classification

Assume:

1. \(E_{\mu\nu}\) is local;
2. \(E_{\mu\nu}\) depends on \(g_{\mu\nu}\), matter fields, and finitely many derivatives;
3. \(E_{\mu\nu}\) is diffeomorphism covariant;
4. \(E_{\mu\nu}\) contains at most second derivatives of the metric in the low-energy regime;
5. \(E_{\mu\nu}\) is divergence-free.

Then, by the Lovelock classification, the gravitational part of \(E_{\mu\nu}\) must be
\[
E^{\mathrm{grav}}_{\mu\nu}
=
\frac{1}{16\pi G}
\left(
G_{\mu\nu}
+
\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
\right)
+
\sum_{p\ge2}\alpha_p H^{(p)}_{\mu\nu},
\]
where \(H^{(p)}_{\mu\nu}\) are higher Lovelock tensors.

Including matter,
\[
E_{\mu\nu}
=
\frac{1}{16\pi G}
\left(
G_{\mu\nu}
+
\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
\right)
-
\frac12 T_{\mu\nu}
+
\sum_{p\ge2}\alpha_p H^{(p)}_{\mu\nu}.
\]

Setting \(E_{\mu\nu}=0\) gives
\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=
8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu}
-
16\pi G\sum_{p\ge2}\alpha_p H^{(p)}_{\mu\nu}.
\]

In the infrared, the higher Lovelock terms are suppressed by the recursive scale \(\ell_\Gamma\). Therefore one recovers Einstein gravity.

Let

=
c_G\,
\Gamma^{ab}{}_{cd}
\Gamma^{cd}{}_{ab},
\]
and
\[
\Lambda
=
c_\Lambda\,
\mathrm{tr}_0(\Gamma),
\]
where \(c_G,c_\Lambda\) are dimensionless coefficients fixed by the normalization of the growth measure.

Thus gravitational coupling and cosmological constant are derived from the recursive law.

### 7.2 Cantorian saturation and the bounce

Cantorian non-collapse forbids exact compression of new structure into old structure. In a finite spatial region, this implies a maximal recursive cell density \(n_*\). Let \(n\) be the effective density of generated cells. The simplest local growth law compatible with finite generation and Cantorian saturation is logistic:
\[
n_{\alpha+1}
=

+

\left(

\right).
\]

In the continuum limit,
\[
\dot n
=

\left(

If the physical energy density is proportional to the active growth density,
\[
\rho=\rho_*\,\frac{n}{n_*},
\]
then the Friedmann equation is modified to
\[
H^2
=
\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho
\left(
1-\frac{\rho}{\rho_*}
\right)
+
\frac{\Lambda}{3}.
\]

At \(\rho=\rho_*\), the effective gravitational density vanishes and the universe bounces.

Thus the bounce is not introduced ad hoc. It follows from Cantorian saturation of recursive growth.
